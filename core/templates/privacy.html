Privacy Policy

Your data, your rights

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

EFXCAPITALS ("we", "our", "us") provides a structured oil and gas investment platform with wallet-based funding and transaction records. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you hold over it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, email, username, WhatsApp number, and password.
  • Financial records — deposit amounts, wallet addresses, transaction hashes, withdrawal requests, and investment activity.
  • Transaction records — wallet addresses, transaction hashes, and review history for deposits and withdrawals.
  • Technical data — device type, browser, and basic usage patterns for security and performance.

3. How we use your data

  • Operate your account, wallet balance, and investment packages.
  • Process and review deposits and withdrawals using transaction records and account history.
  • Prevent fraud, money laundering, and unauthorized access.
  • Contact you about account activity, support, and essential updates.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is only shared where required to operate the platform — such as fraud-prevention or payment-processing services — or where we are legally obligated to do so.

5. Data security

Protected accounts. Authentication, session handling, and account role separation are built into the application. Deposit and withdrawal review history is available to authorized user and admin views to make operations easier to validate later.

6. Your rights

  • Access and export your account data.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect your session. No third-party advertising cookies are used on this platform.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Help Center or WhatsApp support channels available on the site and dashboard.
